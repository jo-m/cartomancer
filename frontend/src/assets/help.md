# Help

## Uploading Tracks

You can upload GPX or FIT files from the **Upload** page. Tracks are processed and displayed on a map.

## Viewing Tracks

Browse all public tracks on the **Tracks** page, or manage your own under **My Tracks**.

When viewing a track, use the controls above the map to set a **start time** and **speed**.
These are used to generate a weather forecast along the route and estimate sun exposure.

## Sun Exposure

The **Sun Intensity** gauge appears below the weather charts when forecast data is available.
It shows how much sun you can expect during your ride on a scale of 0 to 16.

- **0 rays** — You will be riding in the dark/dawn or under very heavy cloud cover.
- **4–8 rays** — Moderate sun, typical of an overcast day or a short ride in clear weather.
- **12–16 rays** — A multi-hour ride on a clear summer day will fill all 16 rays.

The number is calculated from weather forecast data by summing up the solar radiation hitting the ground at each point along your route over the duration of the ride.

Below the gauge, the **Dose** label shows the total solar energy per square metre you would receive, expressed in MJ/m^2, kJ/m^2, or J/m^2.

**This is a rough estimate only. Use it as a general guide rather than a precise measurement.**

### Sun Events

The weather charts also mark **dawn**, **sunrise**, **sunset**, and **dusk** as vertical lines positioned along the route:

| Colour | Line style | Meaning |
| ------ | ---------- | ------- |
| Amber  | Dashed     | Dawn (start of twilight) |
| Amber  | Solid      | Sunrise |
| Purple | Solid      | Sunset |
| Purple | Dashed     | Dusk (end of twilight) |
